WHAT WE DETECT

Each camera gets only the analytics that fit its room

Crowd & Movement

Crowding at entry and outside · fewer than 5 in a lab · empty room · entry / exit during the exam · intruder in a closed room

Invigilators

Invigilator absent from the room · standing still for 7 minutes · too few candidates with the invigilator

Malpractice

Mobile phone in the exam hall · two candidates too close, with repeat counting for the same pair

Equipment

Exam computer moved, lifted or carried away · question-paper box missing · damaged or missing furniture

Secure Rooms

More people than allowed in the server room · movement in the strong room · area hidden from the camera

Camera Health

Room type found automatically · camera not reachable · view blocked, blurred or turned upside down
