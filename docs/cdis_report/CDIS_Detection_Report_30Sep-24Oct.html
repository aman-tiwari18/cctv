4 · Per-day detail

Method

  • Source: CDIS database (camera_report_rtsp), SSC CGL 2026 (exam 42), only shifts marked running in the exam slot schedule. On 30 Sep only Shifts 2 and 3 ran.
  • Table 1 (client view) counts every detection pushed to the client, the same basis as the CDIS columns of the Superview vs CDIS report. Accepted = accepted by the client; Rejected = removed by the client without acceptance; Pending = everything else. Phone is split into Inside Lab / Server Room by the class the camera had in that shift (camera classification history), so cameras reclassified between days are counted correctly; 51 of 860 pushed phone detections have no history row and use the camera's current class.
  • Table 2 (internal) counts only detections inside each script's scheduled window for that shift (from the shift's script assignments; scripts with no window that day, e.g. Intruder, count all their detections). Pushed = sent to the client by an operator; Not pushed = filtered out. % removed after push = removed ÷ pushed.
  • The internal tables shared for 8, 9, 10, 12, 13 and 14 Oct were taken without the window filter, so their totals are higher than here; recomputed without the filter they match the database row for row. The window-filtered tables (30 Sep, 1, 5, 6, 7, 15, 16, 22, 23, 24 Oct) match to within 1–5 alerts per row (window-edge seconds).
  • No exam ran from 17 to 21 Oct; 22 Oct follows 16 Oct directly in the charts.
  • 23 Oct: CDIS Shift 3 did not run (all its script assignments stayed Pending or Failed, 0 detections), so 23 Oct covers Shifts 1 and 2 only.
  • The client keeps reviewing older detections, so accepted / removed counts for a day can grow after that day's tables were shared (e.g. 16 Oct accepted rose from 76 to 411 by 26 Oct). Pushed and total counts do not change.
  • The Shift 1 script schedule for past dates was re-saved after 22 Oct (Scene Classification moved to 05:59–06:24, and the 15 and 16 Oct Intruder rows were dropped). Windows for 30 Sep – 16 Oct are taken from the export of 22 Oct, before that change, so they reflect what actually ran.
  • 15 Oct: the Intruder script was scheduled 05:00–06:00 but kept running until about 11:00, raising 54,204 alerts outside its window (none pushed). They are excluded here, so Intruder shows 6 alerts that day.
  • 5 Oct: the Superview vs CDIS report for that day left out 9 pushed “Less than 5” detections from Shift 1 (10:02–10:35). They are pushed in the database, so this report counts them (171 instead of 162).
  • Client actions (accept / remove) are as recorded in the database on the generation date, so a few counts are higher than in tables shared on the day (e.g. 14 Oct proximity pushed 245 here vs 239 in the 18:11 comparison report).
  • 31 detections were accepted by the client and later removed (8 on 13 Oct, 8 on 16 Oct, 1 on 22 Oct, 3 on 23 Oct, 11 on 24 Oct). Table 1 counts them as Accepted; Table 2 counts them under Removed by client after push.
