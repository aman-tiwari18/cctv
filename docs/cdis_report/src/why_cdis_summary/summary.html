1Camera status: more cameras online for the whole exam

92.4% of CDIS cameras were online for the whole shift. For Superview it was 45.7%.
  • Superview's 95.5% "online" includes cameras that dropped out. Superview counts a camera as online even if it went offline during the shift. Of its cameras, 45.7% stayed online for the whole shift and 49.7% were only partly online: they went offline at some point. Together that makes its 95.5%.
  • Only half of Superview's cameras watched the whole exam. Nearly one in two Superview cameras had a gap in coverage. CDIS kept 92.4% of the same cameras online for the whole shift.
  • Twice as many cameras watching the whole exam. On average CDIS had 3,719 cameras online for the whole shift; Superview had 1,832. That is 2.0 times as many.
  • Same cameras compared. Both systems are compared on the same 4,015 cameras in 113 centres per shift on average: the cameras Superview scheduled for each session, all of which are also in the CDIS camera list.
  • Steady every shift. CDIS was between 87.3% and 95.6% online for the whole shift in every shift it ran. Superview was between 28.8% and 56.5%.

2.1Necessary, true-positive events: incidents only CDIS found

The client confirmed 2,322 CDIS alerts against 288 Superview events: 8.1 times as many true positives.
  • Incidents Superview missed. 2,191 of CDIS's accepted alerts (94.4%) were incidents Superview did not report at all. Only 253 accepted Superview events had no CDIS alert.
  • Net gain. For every confirmed incident only Superview caught, CDIS caught 8.7 that Superview missed.
  • Fewer, more useful alerts. CDIS sent 28,096 alerts against Superview's 48,657 (42% fewer), yet delivered 8.1 times the confirmed incidents.
Superview figures are from its own camera report for each session: online whole shift, partly online (offline for part of the shift) and offline for the whole shift. CDIS online = cameras CDIS reached and checked in the shift. Averages are per shift over the 17 shifts (CDIS online: the 16 shifts it ran).

3Accuracy: a higher acceptance rate at the client

8.3% of CDIS alerts were accepted by the client, against 0.6% of Superview's: about 14 times more accurate.
  • Every shift. CDIS had the higher acceptance rate in 16 of 16 day-shifts it ran: 4.3%–13.4%, against 0.1%–1.1% for Superview.
  • Every analytic. Higher acceptance rate in 10 of 10 analytics both systems run, e.g. Invigilator Static 55.5% vs 21.0%, Phone (Server Room) 20.4% vs 1.2%, Phone (Inside Lab) 13.8% vs 0.0%, Server Room Threshold 11.7% vs 1.5%.
  • Less review work. The client reviewed 12 CDIS alerts per confirmed incident, against 169 Superview events, 14 times less effort per finding.
  • Improving. CDIS reached its best rates on the last exam day: 13.4% (28 Oct Shift 2) and 10.9% for the day.
