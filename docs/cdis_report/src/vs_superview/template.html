4 · Per-day detail

Method

  • Source: the daily Superview vs CDIS comparison reports (docs/comparison_report), one per exam day, SSC CGL 2026 (exam 42). Day figures are the sum of that day's shifts.
  • Superview = every event in Superview's event export for the session. CDIS = detections pushed to the client. Accepted / Pending / Rejected are each system's own client actions as recorded when that day's comparison report was built; the client may have reviewed more since.
  • Superview camera figures come from one dashboard snapshot per day, taken after the sessions (or later, since the dashboard keeps no history), so all shifts of a day share them. CDIS online = cameras for which CDIS captured and classified a frame in the shift.
  • CDIS configured cameras are 3,141 (41 centres) up to 15 Oct and 5,969 (157 centres) from 16 Oct, as recorded in the comparison reports.
  • Cameras reported by both and same-event matches (same camera, same script, within ±3 minutes) are per shift; camera counts are averaged over shifts because a camera can appear in several shifts.
  • 30 Sep: Shift 1 did not run. 7 Oct Shift 3: CDIS online cameras not available. 23 Oct Shift 3: CDIS did not run (0 detections), so Superview is compared against nothing for that shift. No exam ran from 17 to 21 Oct.
